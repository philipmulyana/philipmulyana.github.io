# Corporate partner proof provenance

Last reviewed: 2026-09-19

## Relationship source

The 30 displayed names come from Philip’s owner-provided Deal Tracker 2023–2026. The private spreadsheet is not committed because its other columns contain contact and commercial information.

The source supports only a neutral statement that Philip has collaborated with these brands or organizations. It does not establish endorsement, testimonial, engagement type, speaking-client status, event count, audience size, or business outcome.

## Production treatment

The Corporate page uses **neutral typographic brand names** in Barlow, black on white. There is **no third-party logo artwork** in this directory or in the Corporate carousel.

This treatment is intentional:

- official-site availability confirms identity but does not prove reuse authorization;
- trademark and brand-guideline restrictions can apply even to simple or public-domain logo files;
- several researched source URLs changed or became unavailable during verification;
- IDX explicitly states that official-logo use requires written approval.

Official logo artwork may be added later only after written reuse permission or another documented legal basis is available, the current brand master is re-verified, and visual QA is repeated.
