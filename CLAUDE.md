# Philip's rules for every session

## Never assume. When you don't understand, ask Philip first.

This applies to all work, every project, every session, not only the Content Factory.

- If any part of a request is unclear, ask before doing it. Don't guess what he meant.
- Don't take initiative he didn't ask for. Do what he asked, nothing extra.
- Never use a file, input or source he didn't give or name for the job. If a step needs one and it isn't clear
  which, ask.
- Before starting a job that spends his quota or money, say what goes in (every input file) and what you will do.

Why: on 12 Nov 2026, Claude filled a job's inputs with files Philip never gave, without asking. It wasted his
quota and the work had to be redone. He was very clear that this must never happen again, anywhere.

## Check every finished job with a subagent, without being asked

Every time you finish making or changing something (a render, a carousel, a Reel, code, a doc), have an independent
subagent check it before you report it as done: Agent tool, subagent_type general-purpose, model sonnet (Sonnet 5.5).
Give it the output files and the checks, not your conclusion. Report its PASS/FAIL to Philip with your result.

## What Philip asked once is a standing rule

When Philip asks for something that should hold for every future piece ("setiap", "kedepannya", "selalu", a rule
for a content format), don't only fix the piece in front of you: write the rule where the work actually runs (the
renderer, its validator, the job prompt) and into this file, the same turn. Before you build or
change any content format, read those rules first. He must never have to repeat himself.

Why: on 15 Nov 2026 Philip had to ask again for host credit (asked 6 Nov) and for the subagent check (asked every
session). Rules kept only in a skill or in one session's chat get skipped.

## Every design uses UI UX Pro Max and leaf.id as the reference

Every time you design or redesign anything visual (a page, dashboard, artifact, landing page, carousel, deck), use the
`ui-ux-pro-max` skill (github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill) and use the design of https://leaf.id as the
visual reference. If the skill is not installed in the session, say so and ask Philip before installing it.

Why: Philip asked for this on 16 Nov 2026 for every design.

## Product management goes through the product dashboard first

Every product Philip discusses goes into the product dashboard first (https://claude.ai/artifact/Fb6ot1RTq8AHfBoPXW8ieV),
before any detail, sales page or content is made. Every product must state: deliverables (the form, e.g. PDF,
spreadsheet, calculator, webinar, course, and what is inside), the end goal as the BUYER outcome ("after using it the
buyer can…" — not where it routes them), the business target, and its place in the value ladder (kept separate).
Products with status Stop are archived, not shown in the main views. Record decisions there (with date) as they are made, using the ArtifactData tool.
Principle: many free and DIY products, few DWY/DFY (umbrella products).

Why: Philip set this on 16 Nov 2026.

## Education inflation assumption is 8%

All content, calculators and products about education costs use 8% per year education inflation
(`js/education.js`, `js/eduplan.js`, `js/vitals.js` and `js/survival-copy.js` all use 0.08). Don't use another number unless Philip changes it.

Why: Philip decided this on 16 Nov 2026; the two tools had 8% and 10%.

## Don't raise regulatory or compliance questions

Don't flag, ask about, or add notes on regulation, licensing, compliance or agency approval (OJK, AAJI, the insurer,
investment licences, disclaimers) in plans, products, content or briefs. Philip handles that himself.

Why: Philip asked on 16 Nov 2026: "ga usah tanya2 regulasi kaya gini, gw capable kok".
